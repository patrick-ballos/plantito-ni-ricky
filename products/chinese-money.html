Chinese Money Plant

Product Description

The Chinese Money Plant (Pilea peperomioides) is a striking indoor favorite famous for its vibrant, round, coin-shaped leaves. Often associated with good fortune and prosperity, it creates a unique focal point in any living space.

It grows rapidly during warmer seasons and produces small 'offshoots' or plant babies around its base, which can easily be repotted and shared with friends and family.
