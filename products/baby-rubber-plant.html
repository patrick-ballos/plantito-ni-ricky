Baby Rubber Plant

Product Description

The Baby Rubber Plant (Peperomia obtusifolia) is a popular, compact houseplant known for its thick, glossy, spoon-shaped leaves. It is remarkably resilient, making it an excellent choice for beginner plant lovers or busy office environments.

This lush green plant thrives in medium-to-bright indirect light and helps clean indoor air while adding a fresh, modern aesthetic to any desk or shelf setup.
