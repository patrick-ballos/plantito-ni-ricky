Aloe Vera

Product Description

Aloe Vera is an easy-to-care-for succulent plant species known worldwide for its agricultural and medicinal uses. Its thick, fleshy green leaves store water, making it extremely drought-tolerant and perfect for indoor desks, windowsills, or patio gardens.

Our Aloe Vera plants are organically grown, potted in well-draining soil mixes, and come ready to thrive in bright, indirect sunlight.
